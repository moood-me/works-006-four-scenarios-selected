# 2026-10-29 — Brazilian Portuguese (`pt`)

Why: Павел: «Португальский (бразильский) еще запусти» — a further language, after the nine on `languages`.

- Branch `pt`, from `languages`. The four worlds and four games are in Brazilian Portuguese too: `"pt"` in their
  `languages`; `lang/pt.json`, each scene's `scenes/<folder>/lang/pt.json`, `modules/chart.pt.json`, the games'
  `bespoke/lang/pt/strings.json`. Translated from the English, the Russian read beside it; «você» for the player;
  spoken lines in natural spoken Brazilian Portuguese; choices in the infinitive.
- «Pequenas Coisas Como Estas» (Keegan): Bill Furlong, Eileen, Sara Redmond, a Madre Superiora (who says «o senhor,
  sr. Furlong»), a sra. Kehoe, a sra. Wilson; the convent's laundry, the coal shed; «the guards» — «a polícia»; the
  picture notes «Na realidade: …».
- «O Livro do Verão» (Jansson): Sophia, a Avó («vovó» to Sophia), Papai; the player is the grandmother — feminine
  agreement; the lullaby translated to be singable.
- «Papai Moomin e o Mar» (Jansson; no Brazilian edition known — the title is a rendering of its own): Papai and Mamãe
  Moomin, Moomintroll, My, a Groke, o Pescador; the keeper's diary on the wall and the game's quotes of it word for
  word the same.
- «O Pescador e Sua Alma» (Wilde): o Pescador, a Alma (feminine), a Sereia, a Bruxa, o Padre, o povo do mar.
