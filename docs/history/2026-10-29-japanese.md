# works-006: Japanese (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: ещё язык — японский». Branch `ja`, beside English (the source) and Russian.

## What

- All four worlds and their games declare `"languages": ["en", "ru", "ja"]` and have their `ja` files: the worlds'
  `lang/ja.json`, scenes' `lang/ja.json`, `modules/chart.ja.json` (Moominpappa at Sea), the games'
  `bespoke/lang/ja/strings.json`. The games have no other data by language.
- Translated from the English, the Russian read beside it. Order: Moominpappa at Sea, The Summer Book, The Fisherman
  and His Soul, Small Things Like These (each world, then its game).
- Names as the Japanese editions have them:
  - 『ムーミンパパ海へいく』 (小野寺百合子訳): ムーミンパパ, ムーミンママ, ムーミントロール, ちびのミイ (ミイ in short),
    モラン (the Groke), 漁師, ムーミン谷, 灯台の島. The boat: 冒険号 (not checked against the edition).
  - 『少女ソフィアの夏』 (渡部翠訳): ソフィア, おばあさん in narration and labels, おばあちゃん when Sophia speaks to
    her (as quoted from the book: 「おばあちゃん、いつ死ぬの？」), パパ; Papa calls her 母さん; エリクソン.
  - 「漁師とその魂」 (Wilde): 漁師, 魂, 人魚, 魔女, 司祭 (addressed 神父さま), 陶工, やもめ.
  - 『ほんのささやかなこと』 (鴻巣友季子訳): ビル・ファーロング, アイリーン, セァラ, ネッド, ウィルソン夫人 (as the
    edition's readers quote them); 修道院長 (addressed 院長さま); ケホー夫人, ドハティ夫人, シスター・バーナデット,
    and the daughters キャスリーン, シーラ, ロレッタ, グレース, ジョーン.
