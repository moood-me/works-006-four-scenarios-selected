# works-006: Korean (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: и корейский» — Korean (`ko`) as a further language. Branch `ko`, beside English (the source) and the
other languages.

## What

- All four worlds and their games declare `"ko"` at the end of `"languages"` and have their `ko` files: the worlds'
  `lang/ko.json`, scenes' `lang/ko.json`, `modules/chart.ko.json` (Moominpappa at Sea), the games'
  `bespoke/lang/ko/strings.json`. The games have no other data by language.
- Translated from the English, the Russian read beside it. Order: Moominpappa at Sea, The Summer Book, The Fisherman
  and His Soul, Small Things Like These (each world, then its game).
- Register: narration in the plain written style (해라체); choices, hints and the game interface in 해요체; each
  character in their own voice — children and family 반말, to elders and strangers 존댓말, old-fashioned 하오체/하게체
  where it fits (the Fisherman of Moominpappa at Sea, Papa speaking to him, Eriksson, the potter, the widow, the priest).
- Names and titles:
  - 『무민파파와 바다』 (the Korean edition's title, 작가정신): 무민파파, 무민마마, 무민트롤, 미이 (꼬마 미이), 그로크
    (the Groke), 어부, 무민 골짜기, 등대섬; the boat 모험호. Moomintroll calls his parents 아빠/엄마, My calls Papa 파파.
    Paraffin is 등유.
  - 『여름의 책』 (the Korean edition, 민음사): 소피아, 할머니, 아빠 (in narration and labels too), 에릭손. Papa speaks to
    his mother in 해요체 and calls her 어머니.
  - 「어부와 그의 영혼」 (Wilde): 어부, 영혼, 인어, 마녀, 사제 (addressed 신부님), 도공, 과부, 바다의 족속.
  - 『이처럼 사소한 것들』 (Keegan): 빌 펄롱, 아일린, 미시즈 윌슨 (as the Korean edition's readers quote them);
    transcribed: 세라 (Sara) 레드먼드, 미시즈 케호, 미시즈 도허티, 미스터 케니, 네드, 하위 과부, the daughters 캐슬린,
    쉴라, 로레타, 그레이스, 조운; 수녀원장 (addressed 원장 수녀님), 베르나데트 수녀, 머피 신부; 웩스퍼드.
