# works-006: German (2026-10-29)

Pavel: «ещё язык — немецкий». moood (its `check`) knows `de` now; this repository is translated into it, on branch `de`.

## What

- **Worlds** — `moominpappa-at-sea`, `small-things-like-these`, `the-fisherman-and-his-soul`, `the-summer-book`:
  `"de"` in `"languages"`; every scene's `lang/de.json`, the module `modules/chart.de.json`, each world's
  `lang/de.json`.
- **Games** — the four on them: `"de"` in `"languages"`; `bespoke/lang/de/strings.json` (people's `short` included).
  The games keep no other data by language.
- About 2000 texts. Translated from the English, with the Russian as a second view of meaning and tone. The player
  is «du»; a play is a «Partie»; German quotes „…“ in prose; placeholders, inline tags and `\n` as written. No game
  counts or compares the words of its texts (read in the code first), so none had to stay as it was.

## Checked

`check` on the whole repository: «all right — 2043 texts in 43 scopes»; German is in every world and game.

## Names

- **Moominpappa at Sea** — «Muminvater und das Meer»; Muminvater (in the game Papa, as in English), Mama, Mumintroll,
  My, die Morra (the Groke), das Mumintal; the Fisherman — der Fischer; the keepers — Wärter.
- **Small Things Like These** — «Kleine Dinge wie diese» (Claire Keegan's German edition); the Irish names and
  forms of address as they are (Mrs Kehoe, Mr Furlong, Mammy, Daddy, Father Murphy, the Garda); the Mother Superior —
  die Mutter Oberin, addressed «Mutter».
- **The Fisherman and His Soul** — «Der Fischer und seine Seele» (Wilde's German title); die Seele (feminine, as in
  Russian), die Meerjungfrau, die Hexe, der Priester, der Töpfer; the field where linen is bleached — das Bleichfeld.
- **The Summer Book** — «Das Sommerbuch»; Sophia, Großmutter, Papa, Eriksson; the cat — der Kater.
