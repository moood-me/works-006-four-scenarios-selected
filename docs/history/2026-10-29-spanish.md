# works-006: Spanish (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: ещё язык — испанский». A third language beside English (the source) and Russian: `es`.

## What

- The four worlds and four games — `moominpappa-at-sea`, `small-things-like-these`, `the-fisherman-and-his-soul`,
  `the-summer-book` — declare `"languages": ["en", "ru", "es"]`.
- Laid out by `check --write`, then translated (from the English, the Russian as a second view): each world's
  `lang/es.json`, its scenes' `lang/es.json`, `modules/chart.es.json`, each game's `bespoke/lang/es/strings.json`.
  The games have no other data by language.
- Names as Spanish editions have them: «Papá Mumin y el mar» (Papá Mumin, Mamá Mumin, Mumintroll, My, la Groke, el
  valle de los Mumin); «Cosas pequeñas como esas» (Bill Furlong, Eileen, Sara, la madre superiora); «El pescador y
  su alma» (el Pescador, el Alma, la Sirena, la Bruja, el Sacerdote); «El libro del verano» (Sophia, la Abuela).
  Neutral Spanish, «tú» for the player.

## Checked

moood's `check` (branch `es` of moood): "all right" for the whole repository. The games' own `bespoke/check/run.mjs`
read only the source texts, so they are not affected.
