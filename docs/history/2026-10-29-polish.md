# 2026-10-29 — Polish (`pl`)

Why: Павел: «и польский» — Polish as a further language beside the nine.

- The four games and their worlds: `"pl"` in `languages`, every world's and scene's `lang/pl.json` (and the chart
  module's `modules/chart.pl.json`), each game's `bespoke/lang/pl/strings.json` (1860 texts).
- Titles and names as the Polish editions have them: «Tatuś Muminka i morze» (Tatuś Muminka, Mama Muminka, Muminek,
  Mała Mi, Buka, Dolina Muminków; the lighthouse — «latarnia», feminine), «Księga lata» (Sophia, Babcia, Tata),
  «Drobiazgi takie jak te» (Czarne, 2022: Bill Furlong, Eileen, Sara Redmond, siostra Bernadetta, Matka Przełożona),
  «Rybak i jego dusza» (Rybak, Dusza, Syrenka, Czarownica, Kapłan).
- Register: the player is «ty». Characters address each other as Polish speech of their world would: Tatuś Muminka
  says «pan» to the Fisherman; the nuns say «panie Furlong», Sara says «pan» to Bill; the Fisherman's tale is «ty»
  throughout. The replay and map words are the same in all four games («powtórka», «Rozegraj rozdział od nowa»,
  «Wróć do głównej rozgrywki»).

How it was checked: moood's `check` (its `pl` branch: `pl` in `i18n/languages.json`), `node packages/check/cli.js <repo> --write`
laid the files out, the translations were written into them, then `check` on the whole repository says «all right».
From the English source, the Russian read beside it as a second view of meaning and tone. Only translations and the
`languages` lists were added; no source text, code or doc was changed.
