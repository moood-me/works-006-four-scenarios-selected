# The House

The glazed veranda of the house on a June morning (C2 of the game's scenario), and through its open glass door the room
with Grandmother's and Sophia's beds and the window onto the cape. Stage 2 (the look): the place, its light and its
three people standing — no motion of people but a look and a reach, no camera moves.

- **The frame.** Everything of the house is laid on the unseen anchor `veranda` at its far corner (across -0.6,
  distance 7, facing 225, raised 0.3 m over the land — its floors clear of the land's own painting under them): in its
  frame x runs right along the back wall, z away behind it. The viewer looks into that corner: on the left the glazed
  wall (`side_wall` — the low boarded wall and the band over the windows —, and `glazing`, the white frames of six
  windows, the glass clear), on the right the house's wall (`back_wall`) with the
  doorway, the open glass `door` and the straw `hat` on its nail by it. `floor`, `ceiling` (a card laid face down).
  `lib` turns the frame to the world's metres and back (`lx`, `lz`, `wa`, `wd`).
- **Through the door** the room: `room` (its far wall and window), `room_floor`, `beds` — lit by `room_day`, the
  daylight at its window.
- **Outside the glazing** the rock falls to the sea (`relief.js`: `lib.ground`, level under the house; the draped plane
  `outside`); the sea, skerries and islets far off; the morning sun on the left (`sky#sun`), coming in through the
  glazing (`sun`, through `glazing`) with the sky's light (`sky_in`), dust in its beams.
- **People** (the world's `cast`): Papa by his table (the papers, the coffee pot, the chair pushed back, his camp bed
  along the back wall), his mug in both hands, looking at Grandmother; Grandmother before the door, looking down at
  Sophia; Sophia beside her under the hat, holding her hand, her eyes on the floor (C2, 1.1: she passes under the hat
  without looking up). The one nearer the viewer looks ahead or aside, never back at one farther off: a look back
  turns the body away.
